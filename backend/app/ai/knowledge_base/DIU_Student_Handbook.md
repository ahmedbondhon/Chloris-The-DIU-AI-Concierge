## 🎓 Academic & Grading System
DIU follows the uniform grading system prescribed by the UGC of Bangladesh.

### Grading Scale (Marks Mapping)
| Numerical Grade (%)  | Letter Grade | Grade Point |
| :---                | :---         | :---        |
| 80% and above       | A+           | 4.00        |
| 75% to <80%         | A            | 3.75        |
| 70% to <75%         | A-           | 3.50        |
| 65% to <70%         | B+           | 3.25        |
| 60% to <65%         | B            | 3.00        |
| 55% to <60%         | B-           | 2.75        |
| 50% to <55%         | C+           | 2.50        |
| 45% to <50%         | C            | 2.25        |
| 40% to <45%         | D            | 2.00        |
| Below 40%           | F            | 0.00        |

### Academic Performance
*   **Minimum CGPA for Graduation:** 2.00.
*   **Probation:** If a student's CGPA falls below 2.00, they are placed on academic probation.
*   **Incomplete (I) Grade:** Assigned if a student misses the final exam but has completed other work. Must be resolved within 3 weeks of the next semester.

---

## 📊 Attendance Policy
*   **The 75% Rule:** Students are generally required to attend at least 75% of classes to be eligible to sit for Final Examinations.
*   **ERP Tracking:** All attendance is recorded digitally via the DIU Smart Student Portal.
*   **Impact:** Poor attendance can lead to being barred from exams or receiving a "Warning" status.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I apply for a course registration?**
**A:** Course registration is done through the Smart Student Portal during the registration window specified in the Academic Calendar.

**Q: Where can I find my exam routine?**
**A:** Exam routines are published on the official DIU website and also available in the student portal under the "Exams" section.

**Q: What is the procedure for a semester drop?**
**A:** You must submit a formal application to the Head of the Department through the student portal, citing valid reasons (medical, personal, etc.).

**Q: Is there a transport facility to the Permanent Campus?**
**A:** Yes, DIU provides a comprehensive bus service from various points in Dhaka (Dhanmondi, Uttara, Mirpur) to Daffodil Smart City.

## 🏢 Faculties & Departments
DIU offers a wide range of academic programs organized under various faculties.

### Faculty of Science and Information Technology (FSIT)
*   **CIS (Computing and Information System):** It is an official department of DIU under FSIT. Blends computer science with business and information systems to provide practical IT solutions for enterprises.
*   **CSE (Computer Science and Engineering):** It is an official department of DIU under FSIT. Advanced software engineering, algorithms, artificial intelligence, data science, and comprehensive systems design.
*   **SWE (Software Engineering):** It is an official department of DIU under FSIT. The core software development lifecycle, software architecture, testing, and modern engineering practices.
*   **MCT (Multimedia and Creative Technology):** It is an official department of DIU under FSIT. Digital media, animation, 3D modeling, game design, and visual communication.
*   **GED (General Educational Development):** It is an official department of DIU under FSIT. Providing foundational educational courses in mathematics, physical sciences, and humanities.

### Faculty of Engineering (FE)
*   **EEE (Electrical and Electronic Engineering):** It is an official department of DIU under FE. Power generation, electronics, telecommunications, and circuit design.
*   **TE (Textile Engineering):** It is an official department of DIU under FE. Textile manufacturing, apparel engineering, wet processing, and fabric design.
*   **CE (Civil Engineering):** It is an official department of DIU under FE. The engineering, construction, and maintenance of physical structures like bridges, roads, and buildings.
*   **AE (Architecture):** It is an official department of DIU under FE. The art and science of designing physical structures and modern urban planning.

### Faculty of Business & Entrepreneurship (FBE)
*   **BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration):** It is an official department of DIU under FBE. Management, finance, modern marketing tactics, and entrepreneurial strategies for the corporate world.
*   **THM (Tourism & Hospitality Management):** It is an official department of DIU under FBE. Managing hotels, luxury resorts, commercial travel agencies, and large-scale international events.
*   **RE (Real Estate):** It is an official department of DIU under FBE. Property valuation, urban land management, and real estate business dynamics.
*   **IE (Innovation & Entrepreneurship):** It is an official department of DIU under FBE. Nurturing rapid startups, business innovation, and corporate venture creation.

### Faculty of Allied Health Sciences (FAHS)
*   **Pharmacy:** It is an official department of DIU under FAHS. Pharmaceutical drug development, pharmacology research, and clinical pharmacy practices.
*   **NFE (Nutrition and Food Engineering):** It is an official department of DIU under FAHS. Food processing, quality control, dietary science, and national nutrition management.
*   **PH (Public Health):** It is an official department of DIU under FAHS. Global community health, epidemiology, and healthcare policy management.

### Faculty of Humanities & Social Science (FHSS)
*   **English:** It is an official department of DIU under FHSS. English literature, linguistics, professional communications, and TESOL.
*   **Law:** It is an official department of DIU under FHSS. Comprehensive legal education covering constitutional law, criminal justice, and corporate law.
*   **JMC (Journalism and Mass Communication):** It is an official department of DIU under FHSS. Media broadcasting, public relations, digital media publishing, and print journalism.
*   **DS (Development Studies):** It is an official department of DIU under FHSS. Socio-economic development, policy analysis, and managing global humanitarian issues.
