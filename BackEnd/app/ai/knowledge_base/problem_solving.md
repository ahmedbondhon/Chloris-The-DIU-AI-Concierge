# DIU Problem Solving Knowledge Base

## 💰 Financial & Fee Policies

### Topic: Late Fee Extension
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Visit Accounts Section**: Go to the Accounts office (DSC or Shukrabad) immediately.
2. **Written Application**: Prepare a formal application addressed to the Registrar explaining your situation.
3. **HoD Recommendation**: Get your Head of Department to sign/recommend the application.
4. **Portal Update**: Once submitted, the Accounts Section will extend your deadline in the Smart Student Portal. 🤍

### Topic: Getting a Scholarship / Full Scholarship
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Check Eligibility**: Ensure you meet the GPA requirements (usually 3.80+ for merit, or specific criteria for Sibling/Female waivers).
2. **Apply via Portal**: Log in to the Smart Student Portal during the registration window and find the "Waiver Application" section.
3. **Submit Documents**: Upload required transcripts or financial proofs.
4. **Interview (For Full/Special Scholarships)**: You may be called for an interview with the Scholarship Committee or the Dean. 🤍

### Topic: Fee Not Registering (System Issue)
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Transaction Proof**: Keep your Bkash/Bank transaction ID and screenshot safe.
2. **The 24-Hour Rule**: Wait at least 24 hours for the system to sync.
3. **Contact Accounts IT**: If it still shows unpaid, email `accountsit@daffodilvarsity.edu.bd` with your Student ID and proof.
4. **Manual Clearance**: Visit the Accounts office for manual verification if the email isn't resolved in 48 hours. 🤍

## 🎓 Academic Procedures

### Topic: Retaking a Semester / Semester Drop
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Academic Counseling**: Discuss your situation with your Academic Mentor first.
2. **Apply in Portal**: Navigate to the "Application" section and select "Semester Drop."
3. **Reasoning**: Provide a valid reason (Medical/Personal) and upload supporting docs.
4. **Clearance**: Ensure all previous semester dues are paid; otherwise, the drop will be rejected. 🤍

### Topic: Retaking a Missed Exam
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Valid Evidence**: Obtain a Medical Certificate (if sick) or official proof of emergency.
2. **Controller Application**: Apply to the Controller of Examinations within 3 working days.
3. **Fee Payment**: Pay the 'Incomplete Exam' fee (usually BDT 500-1000) at the bank.
4. **Routine Check**: Keep an eye on the departmental notice board for the "Special Exam" schedule. 🤍

### Topic: Reaching the Registrar or Dean
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Appointment Request**: Visit your Departmental Coordination Officer (DCO) to request an appointment.
2. **Formal Note**: Bring a short written note stating the "Serious Matter" you need to discuss.
3. **Office Hours**: You will be scheduled during the official Office Hours of the Dean/Registrar.
4. **Be Prepared**: Have your Student ID and all relevant evidence ready before entering. 🤍

## 🎭 Campus Life & Events

### Topic: Hosting an Event at DIU
**Steps to Follow:**
1. **Concept Proposal**: Draft a proposal including objective, date, budget, and guest list.
2. **Departmental Approval**: Get your Head of Department (HoD) to sign off on the concept.
3. **DSA Clearance**: Submit the signed proposal to the Director of Student Affairs (DSA) for final approval.
4. **Logistics Requisition**: Once approved, submit requests for venue, sound, and seating to the IT/Maintenance department. 🤍
