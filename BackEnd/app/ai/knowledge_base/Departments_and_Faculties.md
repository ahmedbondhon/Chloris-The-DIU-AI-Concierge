# 🏛️ Faculties and Departments of DIU

Daffodil International University (DIU) is organized into five main faculties, each housing several specialized departments. Below are the details for each faculty and its respective departments.

---

## 💻 Faculty of Science and Information Technology (FSIT)
The Faculty of Science and Information Technology is the largest and most dynamic faculty at DIU. It focuses on cutting-edge research in artificial intelligence, software development, and digital innovation.

### Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
The **Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)** is headed by **Prof. Dr. Sheak Rashed Haider Noori**. This department is a hub for innovation at DIU, specializing in AI, Data Science, and Cybersecurity. It is the most popular choice for students looking to lead the next generation of tech careers.

### Department of Software Engineering (SWE)
The **Department of Software Engineering (SWE)** is led by **Dr. Imran Mahmud**. It focuses on the professional development of software, including mobile technology, quality assurance, and project management.

### Department of Computing & Information System (CIS)
The **Department of Computing and Information System (CIS)** is headed by **Mr. Md. Sarwar Hossain Mollah**. This department specializes in the practical application of IT systems, database management, and information security, preparing students to manage complex technology infrastructures in the business world.

### Department of Multimedia & Creative Technology (MCT)
The **Department of Multimedia and Creative Technology (MCT)** is led by **Mr. Md. Salah Uddin**. It covers creative domains such as Graphic Design, 2D/3D Animation, and Game Design, blending technology with artistic expression.

### Department of Information Technology & Management (ITM)
The **Department of Information Technology and Management (ITM)** is headed by **Ms. Nusrat Jahan**. This department bridge the gap between business and technology, focusing on Management Information Systems (MIS) and tech-driven business strategy.

### Department of Environmental Science & Disaster Management (ESDM)
The **Department of Environmental Science and Disaster Management (ESDM)** is led by **Prof. Dr. A.B.M Kamal Pasha**. It focuses on ecology, climate change mitigation, and disaster safety protocols.

### Department of Physical Education & Sports Science (PESS)
The **Department of Physical Education and Sports Science (PESS)** is headed by **Dr. Mohammad Sohel**, specializing in sports science, physical therapy, and kinesiology.

## 🏗️ Faculty of Engineering (FE)
The Faculty of Engineering focuses on technical excellence, industrial application, and solving real-world engineering challenges.

### Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE)
The **Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)** is headed by **Md. Dara Abdus Satter**. It focuses on power systems, electronics, and telecommunications.

### Department of Textile Engineering
The **Department of Textile Engineering** is led by **Prof. Dr. Md. Mahbubul Haque**. It is one of the premier departments for textile studies in Bangladesh, focusing on garment manufacturing and textile technology.

### Department of Civil Engineering
The **Department of Civil Engineering** is headed by **Dr. Mohammad Hannan Mahmud Khan**, focusing on structural engineering, urban planning, and sustainable infrastructure.

### Department of Architecture
The **Department of Architecture** is led by **Prof. Md. Khairul Enam**, blending artistic vision with functional design for the built environment.

### Department of Information & Communication Engineering (ICE)
The **Department of Information and Communication Engineering (ICE)** is headed by **Mr. Md. Taslim Arefin**, focusing on networking, communications, and data transmission.

## 🌟 Campus Facilities & Smart City Features

Located at **Daffodil Smart City (DSC)**, the campus offers state-of-the-art infrastructure:

### 🏠 Residential Facilities
- **Male & Female Halls**: 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry, and dining.
- **Eco-Halls**: Sustainable living with rainwater harvesting and solar power.

### 🚌 Transport Services
- Extensive bus fleet covering major routes in Dhaka and surrounding areas.
- Shuttle services within the Smart City.

### 🏃 Sports & Recreation
- Large central field and specialized futsal fields.
- Modern Gymnasium and indoor sports complex.
- **DIU Lake**: Boating and relaxation zones.

### 🎓 Academic & Innovation Support
- **One Student One Laptop**: Program providing laptops to students.
- **Innovation Labs**: Specialized labs for Robotics, Cyber Security, and AI.
- **Career Development Center (CDC)**: Assisting with placements and internships.
- **45+ Student Clubs**: Covering academic, cultural, and social interests.
